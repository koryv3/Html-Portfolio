The Best Movies According to Angela

My top 3 movies of all-time.

F1

"F1 was absolute adrenaline—the insanely authentic racing shots and high-stakes tension make you feel like you're right in the cockpit, leaving you totally hooked from start to finish!"

Venom

"Venom is such a fun, chaotic ride—Tom Hardy's chemistry with the symbiote carries the whole film, turning pure action and dark humor into an absolute blast from start to finish!"

Thor

"Thor is the ultimate blend of cosmic action, epic mythology, and great humor—watching Chris Hemsworth bring the God of Thunder to life with all that raw power and heart is just pure entertainment!"
